### What `un` means

`un` = 1 on Washington Post (i=2), Risk.net (152) and Insurance Journal (153). It is the **revenue-unverified flag**: canonical `Public_Release_Flag = EXCLUDE_UNVERIFIED`. In the explorer it replaces the revenue line with "Revenue estimate unverified" (hover card and History card). In `nodes169.json` the same flag also withheld those rows' name and founding year; the live embed restores both from `public-snapshot.json` (`snapshot.py` asserts the flag matches). It is not the founding-year flag. The only row with no founding year is Radio Jornalera NJ (`fy` null, canonical `Founding_Year = UNKNOWN`), which is the one mark on the History tab's dashed outer ring. Note the three `un` rows are still counted in the 169 and in the above-$10M group, and their revenue still drives band and origin.
