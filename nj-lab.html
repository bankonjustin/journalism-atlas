New Jersey has a news problem. And a creator solution.

New Jersey ranks 49th among all 50 states, with just 5.1 journalists per 100,000 residents. Traditional local coverage has collapsed. But something else has emerged: a layer of independent social-video creators covering communities, school boards, high school sports, restaurant culture, emergency response, and civic life with surprising consistency. The Center for Cooperative Media spent a year finding them. The Atlas mapped them.

Research by Carrie Brown, Ph.D., Tara George, M.A., and Joe Amditis, M.A. — Center for Cooperative Media, Montclair State University, April 2026. Read the full study →
