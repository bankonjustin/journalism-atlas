SmartNews and the Independent Journalism Atlas bring independent journalists into the SmartNews app

A new pilot puts independent journalists’ coverage of the 2026 midterm elections and their aftermath in front of SmartNews readers. It launches with Jonathan Bernstein, Chris Cillizza and Judd Legum, has room for a few more political writers, and is taking applications for future phases that expand toward other topics.

The announcement

[DATELINE], [RELEASE DATE]— SmartNews, a global news discovery company, and the Independent Journalism Atlas, a verified database of independent creator-journalists, today announced a pilot that brings independent journalists’ work into the SmartNews app for the 2026 midterm elections and their aftermath.

Starting [LAUNCH DATE], SmartNews readers will find full-text politics and election coverage from independent writers in a dedicated Independent Voices module on the app’s politics vertical, alongside live election results. Selected pieces may also appear in SmartNews’s For You feed and in push notifications.

The founding cohort includes Jonathan Bernstein, Chris Cillizza and Judd Legum. There is room for a few more political writers in the pilot.

The program is built around independent publishers: creators keep ownership and control, only free content runs, no one may train AI on their work, and every piece links back to the creator’s own publication. [DRAFT]

What comes next

SmartNews and the Atlas intend to expand the program in 2027, adding more independent journalists and creators and moving into other topics. The Atlas is taking applications now, both for the remaining spots in the election pilot and for those future phases. Creators can apply at journalismatlas.com/partners/smartnews.

    How it works for creators

    Creators keep doing what they already do. The Atlas sets up the feed and is each creator’s single contact with SmartNews.

    Your writing, as it is

    Pieces come straight from your existing newsletter feed, in full text. There’s no separate content to produce.

    You stay in control

    It’s non-exclusive and you own your work. Only free content runs, and nothing paywalled goes in without your OK.

    No AI training

    Neither SmartNews nor the Atlas may use your work to train AI.

    Built to grow your list

    Pieces carry a call to action back to your publication, and the Atlas passes along the performance data SmartNews shares. Creators are paid to take part.
