    How we count

    Among the 500 feeds that published in every one of the last four weeks, volume was up 4% on the week before. This cohort leaves out the highest-volume feeds, which cannot qualify while RSS depth caps hide their older posts; it will include them once archived history is merged. Compared with the three weeks before, 108 feeds published this week after a quiet stretch and 36 that had published every week did not.

    Across all monitored feeds, raw volume looks up 48% on the week before. Do not read that as growth: a feed only exposes its latest items, so the older days of a prolific feed are missing and the curve climbs toward today whatever people publish. The cohort keeps the feeds constant, which is why it is the number to read.
