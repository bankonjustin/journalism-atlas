Methodology

Co-op here means a newsroom owned by its workers, its readers, or both: worker co-ops, worker-owned companies, reader co-ops and multi-stakeholder co-ops. Collective means a publication run as a collective by its members. Founder-owned outlets, nonprofits and outlets that only receive reader support are not listed on this page.

Ownership is verified from public statements, usually the organization's own about page, and every listing links to its source with the date we last checked it. If we cannot find a public statement that says how the organization is owned, we do not list it. We also check that each organization has published within roughly the last four months.

This page lists organizations. The main Atlas lists individual journalists, and journalists who work at these organizations are evaluated separately under the Atlas's editorial standards. Where a member of an organization is already in the Atlas, you can follow their name to find them.
