## What was done, and how to commit it (two parts, disjoint files)

**B. The swap itself, one line: `assets/css/variables.css`**
- `--color-accent: var(--color-lime)` → `var(--color-acid)` (comment updated). `--color-hover` stays lime.

Commit A first, then B (plus these docs and the log). The two parts touch different files.

## Text on the new acid fills

Black or step-6 text is required on acid. Measured/declared: black `#000` and step-6 `#303036` on all but three: **`#111111`** on `.form-submit` and `.compare-th--right` in `for-brands.html` (an off-scale near-black; contrast on acid is 17:1, so it passes, but it is not black or step 6). Unmeasured at load (no text at load): the badges and gradient avatar.

**Mismatches (all report-only; none caused by the swap):**
1. **Hover is an olive fill with white text** on 6 of 8 (`#5d7400` / `--color-dark-olive` / `--olive`), and `#8bc400` (an off-palette lime) on `research.html .btn-lime`. v14 forbids an olive hover fill; the hover should be lime `#97d600` with black text. After this swap the buttons rest acid and turn olive on hover, which is the old behavior with a new resting color. Fixing it is the olive-fill change (14 olive fills are listed in the 2-ii report) and is the natural next stop.
2. **No CTA has a focus rule** (only the browser default). v14 requires a 2px olive ring, offset 2px, on light.
3. **No CTA has a disabled rule.**
4. Resting borders are `1.5px solid var(--color-accent-on-light)` (olive) on `.btn-lime`, `.btn-lime-lg`, `.header-cta`, `.clear-filters-top`; olive strokes are allowed on light, but v14's CTA spec shows a plain fill.
