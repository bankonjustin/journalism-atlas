SmartNews and the Independent Journalism Atlas bring independent journalists into the SmartNews app

A new pilot puts independent journalists’ coverage of the 2026 midterm elections and their aftermath in front of SmartNews’s millions of readers. It launches with Jonathan Bernstein, Chris Cillizza and Judd Legum, has room for a few more political writers now, and is taking applications for future phases that will expand to more topics.

NEW YORK and WASHINGTON, Oct. [XX], 2026 — SmartNews, one of the largest news apps in the United States, and the Independent Journalism Atlas, a verified database of independent creator-journalists, today announced a pilot that brings the work of independent journalists directly into the SmartNews app for the 2026 midterm elections and their aftermath.

Starting this week, SmartNews readers will find full-text politics and election coverage from independent writers in a dedicated Independent Voices module on the app’s politics page, alongside live election results. Selected pieces may also appear in SmartNews’s For You feed and in push notifications.

The founding cohort includes political scientist and longtime columnist Jonathan Bernstein, political analyst Chris Cillizza, and Judd Legum, founder of Popular Information. The Atlas will add a small number of additional political writers to the pilot in the coming weeks.

The program is designed around the needs of independent publishers. Creators’ existing work flows in from their own feeds with no extra production, they keep full ownership and editorial control, and they choose which pieces run free in the app. Each piece carries a call to action back to the creator’s own publication, and the Atlas shares performance data from SmartNews with every participant. Participating creators are paid for the pilot, and neither company may use their work to train AI models.

What comes next

SmartNews and the Atlas plan to expand the program in 2027, adding more independent journalists and creators and moving beyond politics into other topics. The Atlas is taking applications now, both for the remaining spots in the election pilot and for those future phases. Creators can apply at journalismatlas.com/partners/smartnews.
