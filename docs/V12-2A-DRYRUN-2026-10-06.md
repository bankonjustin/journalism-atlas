**Method.** Variable definitions in `variables.css` are changed once, to v12 tokens. Everywhere else the hex is replaced by its v12 literal (`#313131`→`#303036`, `#efeff2`→`#efeff4`, `#6b6b6b`→`#5e5e64`; `rgba(49,49,49,a)`→`rgba(48,48,54,a)`, `rgba(239,239,242,a)`→`rgba(239,239,244,a)`; alpha preserved). Literal swaps, not `var()` substitutions, so nothing changes cascade behaviour and each line is auditable. Tokenizing inline styles can be a later cleanup. Inline `:root` definitions (`--page-bg`, `--text`, `--olive`, `--dark`, `--text-primary`, …) are changed at the definition, so their consumers follow with no edits.
