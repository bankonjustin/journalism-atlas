Three ways a brand meets a creator journalist.

As the story.

A creator covers you: favorably, critically, or somewhere in between. You don't choose this. Your only choice is whether you're prepared.

We tell you who covers your issue before they publish. (Monitoring)

As the sponsor.

You underwrite the work. You don't shape the editorial. You're buying association with their credibility, not control of their output. Think NPR underwriting, not native advertising.

We match you with creators whose audiences fit, under our standards. (Partnerships)

As the collaborator.

You co-create something: a series, a live event, a piece of access reporting. The creator keeps editorial independence; you bring something they couldn't make alone.

We structure it so both sides can say yes. (Partnerships)

Your job is to be the brand they say yes to.

What we will and won't do.

We won't

  • Sell coverage. We can tell you who covers your issue. We can't and won't arrange what they say.
  • Share creators' private contact details or data without their consent.
  • Broker undisclosed sponsorships. Every paid relationship we help build is disclosed by the creator, their way.

We will

  • Verify every creator in the Atlas by hand.
  • Tell you when the honest answer is "nobody independent is covering this yet."
  • Tell creators which brands we work with, if they ask.
